Market makers on the book

A limit order book with price-time priority, filled by zero-intelligence order flow from Cont, Stoikov and Talreja (2010) with their rates fitted to Sky Perfect: limit orders arrive at each distance from the opposite best, market orders at 0.94 a minute, and each resting order is cancelled at a rate that depends on how deep it sits. Two market makers quote one lot each, post only. The GLFT bot uses the Guéant, Lehalle and Fernandez-Tapia quotes for an inventory limit of 5; the Avellaneda-Stoikov bot shrinks its spread as the hour runs out. Both fit A = 2.1 and k = 3.6 per minute to this flow.

Unlike the other page, nothing here fills by formula. An order trades when a market order reaches it, which means waiting behind every order that joined the level first. The ladder shows each order as a block in queue order, inside edge on the left.

Two switches make the flow harder. Hawkes market orders arrive in bursts: each one raises the rate of the next on the same side by 0.6 and on the other side by 0.1, decaying over 10 seconds, with the same 0.94 a minute on average. The informed trader watches a value that moves like the mid would (1 tick per square-root minute), checks it about once a minute, and sends a market order whenever the value is outside the best quotes. Turn them on and see which fills hurt: the table at the end splits them by place in the queue.

Real market orders are not one lot. On the NASDAQ day this project replays, 21% of the 83,622 market orders across six symbols were at least one and a half times the average limit order, and their sizes follow a power law, P(k) proportional to k-2.7, much heavier than a geometric fit. With ITCH sizes on, market orders draw from that law (capped at 20 lots) and arrive less often, so the same number of shares trades per minute. A big one can eat a whole level and the one behind it. The last-share switch gives GLFT a defence: when its quote would be alone at its price, or have fewer than two lots from others beside it, it steps one tick back.
